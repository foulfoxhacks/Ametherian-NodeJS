# Code of Conduct

Ametherian aims to be a constructive, technically curious, and respectful project space.

Participants are expected to:

- Be respectful and assume good faith while remaining open to technical disagreement.
- Critique ideas and implementation details, not people.
- Share reproducible evidence when reporting defects or making technical claims.
- Respect privacy, licensing, attribution, and responsible disclosure.
- Avoid harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing, and disruptive behavior.

Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when necessary to protect contributors and the project. A reporting contact will be added when the public repository is established.
