The idea behind Ametherian

Consistency without compromise.

Modern development depends on a wide constellation of packages. Each has its own conventions, assumptions, and lifecycle. Ametherian explores what happens when focused tools share a common philosophy — without forcing developers into a monolith or pretending established projects should disappear.
